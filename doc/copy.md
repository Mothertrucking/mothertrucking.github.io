## Story `/story`

**Meta title:** Our Story — Mothertrucking Apps
**Meta description:** How a NZ software studio got built on cranes, HIAB trucks, and figuring things out the hard way.

---

**H1:** It started with cranes.

**Intro:**
Not in a boardroom. Not with a pitch deck.
We started by working alongside crane operators, HIAB specialists, and lifting companies across New Zealand — trying to understand what they actually needed.

Then we tried to build it all.

---

**Section: The education**

The original Mother Trucking product set out to solve every problem in the lifting and shifting sector.
It didn't. But it taught us which problems were real, which were hard, and which were worth a dedicated product.

That's still how we work. Build fast. Get it in front of real people. Fix the actual problem.

We call it Fluff Around and Find Out. _(inside joke — it means exactly what you think it means)_

---

**Section: The evolution**

Mothertrucking Fleet Management.
Then Mothertrucking Logistics Software.
Now Mothertrucking Apps.

The name follows the understanding. Each version is a more honest description of what this actually is.

---

**Section: Where we are now**

We're a product studio. We build software for industries we understand deeply — starting with transport.
Our enterprise arm, lowcode.co.nz, works with organisations like Michelin on the serious stuff.
Our products come from the same domain knowledge, built to solve the problems that survive the filter.

We're based in New Zealand. We work on the front line.
That's not a marketing line — it's just where the problems are.
