Paleocene Giant

Meet Your New Apex Pet

📏

Massive Scale

Adult females can reach lengths of up to 50 feet (15 meters) and weigh over 2,500 pounds. Proper spatial planning is non-negotiable.

🌡️

Tropical Climate

Hailing from the ancient Cerrejón rainforests, they require ambient temperatures between 86°F and 93°F (30°C - 34°C) to survive.

💧

Semi-Aquatic

Titanoboas spend up to 80% of their lives submerged. Water supports their immense mass and is essential for shedding and hunting.

Habitat Construction

The Biodome

A standard glass terrarium will not suffice. To house a Titanoboa, you must construct a commercial-grade aquatic biodome. The enclosure must mimic the swampy ecosystems of prehistoric Colombia.

  • Minimum Size: 100 x 50 meters of floor space per adult.
  • Water Features: Deep, heated pools (minimum 10ft depth) covering 70% of the enclosure.
  • Filtration: Industrial-grade water filtration capable of processing 50,000 gallons per hour.
  • Security: 3-inch thick reinforced polycarbonate viewing glass.

Piscivorous Diet

Feeding Protocol

Contrary to popular belief, Titanoboa is primarily a fish-eater. Its skull and teeth are uniquely adapted for catching aquatic prey, though it will consume land animals if the opportunity arises.

  • Staple Diet: Large bony fish, ideally ranging from 50 to 150 lbs per fish (e.g., Arapaima, giant lungfish).
  • Feeding Frequency: One massive meal (approx. 200-300 lbs of fish) every 3 to 4 weeks.
  • Supplements: Enriched whole prey items (like farm-raised alligators) can be offered biannually for dietary variety.
  • Caution: Never hand-feed. Always use mechanical crane systems or automated drop chutes.

Risk Management

Handling & Safety Rules

No Free Roaming

Never allow a Titanoboa to free-roam outside its secure biodome. They are ambush predators and will perceive any movement as a potential food source.

The Buddy System

Maintenance within the enclosure requires a minimum team of four trained professionals. Never enter the biodome alone.

Lockdown Protocols

Airlock systems with dual-failsafe electronic locks must be installed on all entry points to prevent accidental escapes.
